Round-based tactical FPS in three.js (make.fun threejs-fps-01): you + 4 bot teammates ("Sparrows", attackers) vs 5 bot defenders ("Jackals") in an abandoned freight yard at dusk. First to 5 round wins. Plant the bomb at site A or B, or eliminate the enemy team. Economy with a buy phase, bot AI with sight/sound perception, recoil/spread gunplay, and a 3-lane map.

Play locally: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
